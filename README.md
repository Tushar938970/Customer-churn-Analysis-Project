# Customer-churn-Analysis-Project
## 📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing customer churn data to understand customer retention, revenue loss, churn patterns, and customer behavior.

The analysis was performed using Python and data analytics techniques to identify important business insights and possible action items for reducing customer churn.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze customer churn and retention
- Identify major churn patterns
- Analyze revenue loss due to customer churn
- Understand monthly and annual churn trends
- Identify states and subscription plans with higher churn
- Calculate important business metrics
- Suggest actionable steps to improve customer retention

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- Python
- Jupyter Notebook
- Pandas
- NumPy
- Matplotlib
- Seaborn
- Data Analysis
- Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)

---

## 📊 Key Insights

### Customer Churn

- **Churn Rate:** 28.6%
- **Retention Rate:** 71.4%

### Customer & Revenue Analysis

- Most of the churn is from the **Basic subscription plan**.
- Most of the churn occurred in **September 2024**.
- **Karnataka** was the most affected state.
- **Average Tenure:** 1,451 days
- **ARPU:** Rs 18.8
- **Total Revenue:** 395
- **Revenue Loss due to Churn:** 74
- **CLTV Lost:** 2,047
- **Revenue Loss:** 18%

### Monthly vs Annual Churn

- **Monthly Churn:** 55.6%
- **Annual Churn:** 8.3%

This shows a significant difference between monthly and annual customer churn.

---

## 🔍 Business Insights

The analysis indicates that customer churn is concentrated in specific subscription plans and periods.

The **Basic subscription plan** accounts for most of the churn, while **September 2024** experienced the highest churn.

**Karnataka** was identified as the most affected state.

Monthly customers show considerably higher churn compared with annual customers.

---

## 💡 Recommended Action Items

Based on the analysis, the following areas can be investigated:

1. Check what happened in **Karnataka**, including:
   - Price changes
   - Customer complaints
   - Technical issues
   - Service-related problems

2. Investigate whether the **Basic plan subscription price** increased or whether any changes were made, particularly around September.

3. Analyze competitor activity because some customers may have moved to competitors.

4. Focus on customers with **High and Medium churn risk**.

5. Check customer **Lifetime Value (LTV)** to prioritize customers who require retention efforts.

6. Contact high-risk customers through:
   - Email
   - SMS
   - Calls

7. Identify and resolve customer issues to improve retention.
